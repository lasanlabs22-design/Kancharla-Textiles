Theme: Mangalagiri arts

Design language: Myntra's structure, Mangalagiri's soul

Navigation, filters, bag and checkout follow the patterns Indian shoppers already know from AJIO and Myntra. The colours, borders, type and motion come from Mangalagiri's temple on the hill and the pit looms in its weaving lanes. This page uses the same palette and motifs, so it doubles as a preview.

Rules for the theme

  • Products come first. Ornament lives in borders, headers and empty states. Product grids and PDP galleries stay clean on lime-white, the way they do on Myntra.
  • Treat sacred imagery with respect. Use the temple's architecture, the hill and the looms. Keep deity images and religious symbols off price tags, discount badges and footers.
  • English only. All text on the site is in English. The Mangalagiri character comes from colour, borders and motifs, not language.
  • Motion stays calm: tiers rise once on first load, and the zari border draws in on hover. Everything respects reduced motion.
  • Festival banners: for launch, the owner swaps hero banners by hand. Scheduled festival themes come after launch.
  • The admin console stays plain. It gets only a kumkum accent and the logo, because the owner needs speed, not decoration.
Locked decisions

Stack decisions

Each choice below was confirmed with the team or follows from one that was.

StorefrontNext.js (App Router) on Cloudflare Workers

Deployed with the OpenNext Cloudflare adapter. Pages are server-rendered at the edge and cached by tag, so the site stays fast in Tier-2 and Tier-3 towns.

Commerce engineMedusa v2 on Railway

Open-source and written in TypeScript. Products, variants, pricing, inventory, carts and orders come built in. We add small custom modules for menus, homepage content, wishlist and store rules.

DatabasePostgreSQL + Redis on Railway

Postgres holds all customer, catalog and order data. Redis carries Medusa's event bus, workflow locks and cache.

Customer loginFirebase phone OTP, at checkout

Anyone can browse and fill a bag. The OTP is asked at checkout, as on AJIO and Myntra. Firebase only proves who owns the number, and the customer record lives in Postgres.

PaymentsPhonePe PG + Cash on delivery

PhonePe Standard Checkout for UPI, cards and netbanking, confirmed by signed callbacks. COD is a second payment option, with the cash collected by Delhivery. The owner can switch COD off in the admin.

LogisticsDelhivery

Pincode serviceability and delivery-date estimates, waybill creation, labels, pickup requests and tracking scans.

MediaCloudflare R2 + Images

R2 stores the original photos with no egress fees. Cloudflare Images serves AVIF/WebP at the sizes each screen needs.

SearchPostgres search at launch

Fine for the launch catalog and keeps Railway in budget. Meilisearch, which adds typo tolerance and synonyms like "pattu" for silk, can be added once the catalog grows.

EmailResend

Order confirmation, shipping and delivery emails, with the GST invoice PDF attached. It sends from orders@kimai.store.

System view

Architecture

Three zones. Cloudflare serves every customer request at the edge. Railway runs the commerce engine and data. Third-party services handle identity, money and parcels. The red dashed lines are how an admin change reaches the live site.

To stay inside the $5–20/month Railway budget, launch runs three services: Medusa (API and background jobs together), Postgres and Redis. When traffic grows, the background jobs move to their own worker service and Meilisearch can be added, with no code changes.

Custom extensionHomepage

Hero banners, "Shop by category" tiles, product rails and testimonials, all edited by hand.

OrdersFulfilment desk

Payment status, one-click Delhivery waybill, label and GST invoice PDFs, pickup requests, cancellation approvals and COD remittance tracking.

AlertsNew-order alerts

Every new order rings a bell in the admin console and sends an SMS to the owner's phone.

SettingsStore rules

Shipping charge (5% at launch), COD on/off, COD order cap and fee, excluded pincodes, GSTIN and invoice details.

Only the owner uses the admin, so there are no staff roles at launch. Sign-in is protected twice: by Cloudflare Access, then by the Medusa login. Coupons are built in but stay off at launch, and there are no custom reports. Medusa's standard order list and dashboard are enough.

Inventory: from the admin to the shelf

  • Adding stock: the owner types in quantities per colour and size for the Mangalagiri store, which is the only stock location.
  • Product pages are always current: stock and price are fetched live and never cached, so a size that's back in stock can be bought straight away.
  • Listing pages update automatically: when an item sells out or comes back, the worker refreshes the cache for that product and its categories, so "Sold out" badges and "In stock" filters update within seconds.
  • Orders move stock by themselves: stock is reserved when an order is placed, deducted when it ships, and returned when a cancellation is approved. Two shoppers cannot buy the last piece.
  • Low stock: items below a threshold get a "Low stock" marker in the admin list. "Notify me when back in stock" comes after launch.

Phone OTP

Customer login

The site is open to everyone. Login is phone OTP only, and it's asked at the start of checkout, or when a shopper opens their account or wishlist. Firebase sends and checks the OTP. Medusa then swaps the Firebase token for its own session, so all customer data stays in our own Postgres.

sequenceDiagram
  actor U as Shopper
  participant B as Browser
  participant F as Firebase Auth
  participant M as Medusa API
  participant D as Postgres
  U->>B: Taps Place order, enters +91 mobile number
  B->>F: signInWithPhoneNumber, with reCAPTCHA and App Check
  F-->>U: SMS with 6-digit OTP
  U->>B: Enters OTP
  B->>F: confirm(code)
  F-->>B: Firebase ID token
  B->>M: POST /auth/customer/firebase with idToken
  M->>F: verifyIdToken using the Admin SDK
  M->>D: Find customer by phone or create one, then link the Firebase uid
  M-->>B: httpOnly secure session cookie
  Note over B,M: Guest bag moves into the account and checkout continues
  • The Firebase step is built as a custom Medusa auth provider, so the rest of Medusa treats it like any other login method.
  • Blaze-plan safety: restrict the SMS region policy to India (+91), enforce App Check, and use Cloudflare rate limits on the login route. This prevents SMS-pumping fraud from running up the bill.
  • Profile is three fields: name, phone (verified by OTP) and email (for order mail and invoices). Nothing else is collected.
  • No marketing messages. Customers receive order emails only, so there's no opt-in step.
  • This is a fresh store, so no customers are imported from WooCommerce.
PhonePe + Delhivery

Checkout & payments

Checkout has three steps: address, delivery and pay. The order is created only after the server has confirmed the payment with PhonePe. A customer who closes the tab after paying still gets the order.

sequenceDiagram
  participant B as Browser
  participant M as Medusa API
  participant DL as Delhivery
  participant P as PhonePe PG
  participant W as Background jobs
  B->>M: Address with pincode
  M->>DL: Serviceability and delivery-date estimate
  DL-->>M: Serviceable, ETA 4 to 6 days
  B->>M: Pay now
  M->>P: Create payment with order id equal to the cart id
  P-->>M: Redirect URL
  B->>P: Pays by UPI, card or netbanking
  P->>M: Signed server-to-server callback
  M->>P: Order status check, never trust the callback alone
  M->>M: Complete cart, create order, reserve stock
  P-->>B: Redirect to the order-confirmed page
  W->>P: Every 5 min, check payments still pending
  • Requests use idempotency keys, so a double tap or a retried callback cannot create two orders.
  • Shipping charge: 5% of the order value, added as its own line. The percentage is set in the admin. There is no free-shipping threshold at launch, and India-wide delivery only.
  • GST invoice: prices include GST. Each product carries an HSN code and tax rate. The invoice PDF prints the GSTIN and Kancharla Textiles' details, is emailed on shipping, and can be downloaded from the account.
  • Cancellations need the owner's approval. The customer taps "Request cancellation" before the order ships, and the owner approves or declines in the admin. If approved, a prepaid order is refunded through PhonePe, and a COD order is cancelled with nothing to refund.
  • No returns or exchanges at launch, as agreed. The policy page will say so clearly.

Cash on delivery

COD is included at launch. It's a separate payment provider in Medusa, so the owner can turn it off later from the admin without a code change or deploy.

  • The owner sets every COD rule in the admin: on/off, maximum order value, COD fee (or none) and excluded pincodes. COD also shows only where Delhivery confirms the pincode accepts cash.
  • Verified phone: the shopper proved their number by OTP at checkout, so there's no extra confirmation step. Repeat refusers can be blocked from COD in the admin.
  • Order flow: a COD order skips PhonePe and goes straight to Confirmed. The waybill is created with the payment mode set to COD and the amount to collect.
  • Reconciliation: Delhivery's COD remittance reports are matched to orders, and the order is marked paid when the cash is remitted. The admin shows which COD amounts are still outstanding.
  • Refused parcels: COD parcels the customer refuses return to the store (RTO, return to origin), and the order is flagged in the admin.
After the sale

Order tracking

The owner creates a Delhivery waybill from the admin in one click. Every courier scan after that updates the customer's timeline, and key steps send an email through Resend.

stateDiagram-v2
  direction LR
  [*] --> Placed
  Placed --> Confirmed: paid online, or COD accepted
  Confirmed --> Packed: waybill created
  Packed --> Shipped: picked up by Delhivery
  Shipped --> OutForDelivery
  OutForDelivery --> Delivered
  Shipped --> RTO: undeliverable
  Confirmed --> CancelRequested: customer asks, before shipping
  CancelRequested --> Cancelled: owner approves, refund if prepaid
  CancelRequested --> Confirmed: owner declines
  • Scans arrive by Delhivery push webhook where the account has it enabled. Otherwise the worker polls the tracking API every 30 minutes for active shipments.
  • Raw scans are saved as shipment_event rows and mapped to the six simple statuses customers see.
  • Customers can track from their account or from a public /track page using order number and phone, with an OTP for full details.
  • Emails go out on Placed, Shipped (with the invoice), Out for delivery, Delivered and Cancelled. The owner gets an SMS and an admin-console alert for every new order and every cancellation request.

Trust

Security & compliance

  • Card data never touches our servers. Card entry happens on PhonePe's hosted checkout, which keeps PCI scope minimal.
  • The admin is invisible to the public. It sits behind Cloudflare Access, so the owner confirms an email code before Medusa's own login screen even loads.
  • Bot protection: WAF managed rules, Turnstile on checkout, and rate limits on OTP and tracking routes.
  • Data protection: only name, phone, email and address are collected. There's no analytics tracking at launch, account deletion on request, and a privacy policy written for India's DPDP Act.
  • Secrets: stored as Railway and Cloudflare environment variables, never in the repo. Webhook signatures are checked on every callback.
  • Backups: Railway volume backups plus a nightly pg_dump to R2, kept for 30 days. Restores are tested every quarter.
  • Monitoring: Sentry's free tier for storefront and API errors, plus an uptime check on checkout.
